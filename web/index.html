      Teach SignSpeak your signs

      The model learned from only four signers, so it may misread letters you form a little differently. Record a few short takes of each letter in front of your own camera, then fine-tune. It takes about 10 minutes and fixes most mix-ups.

      1. 1Record 3 takes of every letter
      2. 2Fine-tune (about a minute)
      3. 3Use your personalised model
      Record all 26 letters, not just the ones it gets wrong. If you record only some letters, the model learns that pictures from your camera are one of those letters, and the letters you didn't record start being misread as them.
